Why Unforget

Stays on your device

Photos, faces, names and documents never leave your phone or PC. No account, no ads, no analytics – it all works offline.

Finds what you’re looking for

Search by content (“tractor”, “sunset”), by person, place and date – and inside the text of your documents.

Documents with deadlines

Invoices, receipts, warranties and contracts: Unforget reads type, amount and sender and reminds you of payments due and warranties expiring.

Phone and PC in sync

Pair with Unforget for Windows by QR code. New photos arrive on the PC like a backup, names and albums sync both ways – directly over your own Wi‑Fi, encrypted.

Download

Windows

  1. Open the latest release and download Unforget-Setup-version.exe (not “Update”).
  2. On first start Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” (the app is new): More info → Run anyway.
